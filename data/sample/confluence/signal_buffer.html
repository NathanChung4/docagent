Signal Buffer

Overview

The signal buffer absorbs short-term mismatches between producer and consumer rates. It is sized for a few hundred milliseconds of typical traffic and will drop the oldest entries when full.

Parameters

  • capacity (int, 16–65536, default 4096) — number of entries the buffer can hold.
  • drop_policy (str, one of "oldest" or "newest", default "oldest") — which entry is dropped when capacity is exceeded.
  • flush_interval_ms (int, 1–10000, default 50) — period at which buffered entries are forwarded downstream.

Validation Procedure

  1. Produce at 2× the sustained consumer rate for 5 seconds.
  2. Confirm dropped-entry count matches the expected (producer_rate − consumer_rate) × duration within 5%.
  3. Confirm drop_policy="oldest" preserves the most recent entries.

Known Issues

Setting capacity above 16384 increases per-entry latency because the underlying ring buffer's index becomes harder to keep in cache. Use only when you genuinely need the depth.
