Clock Divider

Overview

The clock divider derives a slower, jitter-controlled clock from the master reference and supplies it to the rest of the pipeline. All downstream components share whatever clock the divider produces.

Parameters

  • divisor (int, 1–256, default 8) — integer divider applied to the master clock.
  • jitter_budget_ps (int, 10–10000, default 500) — maximum allowed cycle-to-cycle jitter, in picoseconds.
  • phase_offset_deg (float, 0.0–359.9, default 0.0) — phase shift applied to the divided clock relative to the reference.

Validation Procedure

  1. Lock the master reference at its nominal frequency.
  2. Sweep divisor across {1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32} and confirm the output frequency matches reference / divisor within 0.1%.
  3. Measure cycle-to-cycle jitter and confirm it stays under jitter_budget_ps at every divisor setting.

Known Issues

Non-power-of-two divisors (3, 5, 7, …) increase output jitter. If your design needs an odd divisor, raise jitter_budget_ps by at least 50%.
