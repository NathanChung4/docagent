Temperature Regulator

Overview

The temperature regulator monitors the operating temperature of the worker pool and adjusts the active worker count to keep the pool inside its safe thermal envelope. It is most active during sustained high-throughput periods.

Parameters

  • target_temp_c (float, 30.0–80.0, default 65.0) — target steady-state temperature.
  • kp (float, 0.1–10.0, default 1.5) — proportional gain in the regulator's PID loop.
  • ki (float, 0.0–5.0, default 0.3) — integral gain in the PID loop.
  • min_workers (int, 1–32, default 4) — never reduce the worker count below this floor.

Validation Procedure

  1. Run a sustained CPU-bound workload for 10 minutes at default settings.
  2. Confirm temperature stays within ±2°C of target_temp_c for the final 5 minutes.
  3. Sweep kp across {0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 3.0} and capture overshoot and settling time.

Known Issues

Aggressive PID gains (kp > 3.0) can cause the regulator to oscillate the worker count, which in turn causes upstream queues to oscillate. Prefer gentler gains in production.
