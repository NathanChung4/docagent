Voltage Monitor

Overview

The voltage monitor samples the supply rail and emits an alert when the rail drifts outside a configurable tolerance window. It is a passive observer — it never adjusts the rail itself.

Parameters

  • nominal_voltage (float, 0.5–5.0, default 1.2) — expected supply voltage in volts.
  • tolerance_pct (float, 0.1–20.0, default 5.0) — allowed deviation, as a percent of nominal.
  • sample_hz (int, 1–10000, default 1000) — sampling frequency.

Validation Procedure

  1. Inject controlled voltage steps of ±1%, ±5%, and ±10% of nominal.
  2. Confirm the monitor remains silent for steps inside tolerance_pct and alerts within one sample period for steps outside.

Known Issues

The monitor cannot distinguish a true rail event from a noisy probe. If you see frequent false alerts, check probe shielding before adjusting tolerance_pct.
